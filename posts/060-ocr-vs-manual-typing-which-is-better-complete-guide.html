What is OCR?

OCR stands for Optical Character Recognition. It's a technology that "reads" text in images and converts it into machine-readable text.

How it works:

  1. The software analyzes the image
  2. It identifies shapes that look like letters and numbers
  3. It converts those shapes into actual text characters
  4. You get editable, searchable text

Think of it like this: A scanned PDF is like a photograph. OCR is like having someone read that photograph and type out everything they see — but in seconds, not hours.

Method 2: Manual Typing

How It Works

Manual typing is straightforward:

  1. Open the scanned PDF in a viewer
  2. Place the PDF and your word processor side by side
  3. Read the text and type it into your document
  4. Check for typos and formatting issues

Hybrid Approach: The Best of Both Worlds

The most practical approach combines both methods:

  1. Run OCR on your scanned document
  2. Review the output for errors
  3. Manually correct misrecognized characters
  4. Proofread the final text

This approach gives you:

  • The speed of OCR (95% of the work)
  • The accuracy of manual review (100% of the result)
