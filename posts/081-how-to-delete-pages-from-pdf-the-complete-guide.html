How to Delete Pages from PDF: The Complete Guide

You have a PDF with pages you don't need. Maybe a blank page snuck in. Maybe you scanned extra pages by mistake. Or maybe you want to remove confidential information before sharing.

Whatever the reason, deleting pages from a PDF is easy. This complete guide shows you how.

Why Delete Pages from PDF?

Deleting pages is one of the most common PDF tasks. Here's why people do it:

Scenario Why Delete
Remove blanks Delete empty pages that waste space
Fix scanning errors Remove duplicate or mis-scanned pages
Protect privacy Delete sensitive information before sharing
Shorten documents Keep only the pages you need
Improve file size Fewer pages = smaller file
Prepare for sharing Send only relevant sections

Delete vs. Extract: What's the Difference?

Many people confuse deleting with extracting. Here's the difference:

Feature Delete Pages Extract Pages
What it does Removes unwanted pages Saves selected pages as new file
Original file Becomes smaller Remains unchanged
Output Same file, fewer pages New file with selected pages
Best for Removing mistakes Saving specific pages

Simple rule: Delete removes pages from the file. Extract creates a new file with only selected pages.

Method 2: Adobe Acrobat Pro (Desktop)

Adobe Acrobat Pro offers the highest quality page deletion with full control over settings.

How it works:

  1. Open the PDF in Acrobat Pro
  2. Click Tools > Organize Pages
  3. Select the pages you want to delete
  4. Click the Delete button (trash icon)
  5. Confirm deletion
  6. Save the PDF

Pros:

  • Highest quality output
  • Full control over page selection
  • Works offline
  • Batch processing
  • Thumbnail preview

Cons:

  • Expensive (subscription required)
  • Installation required

When to use: Professional use, complex documents, sensitive files.

Method 3: Preview (Mac) — Built-in Tool

Mac users can use Preview for quick PDF page deletion.

How it works:

  1. Open your PDF in Preview
  2. Open the thumbnail sidebar (View > Thumbnails)
  3. Select the pages you want to delete
  4. Press Delete on your keyboard
  5. Save the file

Pros:

  • Free with Mac
  • Simple and fast
  • Works offline
  • Thumbnail preview

Cons:

  • One page at a time
  • No batch processing for multiple files
  • Limited to basic deletion

When to use: Mac users, quick page deletion.

Method 4: Microsoft Word (Windows)

Windows users can use Microsoft Word to delete PDF pages.

How it works:

  1. Open the PDF in Word (converts to editable document)
  2. Delete the pages you don't need
  3. Save as PDF

Pros:

  • Free with Word
  • Works offline

Cons:

  • PDF conversion may change formatting
  • Not ideal for complex PDFs
  • Manual cleanup may be needed

When to use: Windows users, simple documents.

Method 5: Google Drive (Free Cloud-Based)

Google Drive offers a free way to delete PDF pages.

How it works:

  1. Upload your PDF to Google Drive
  2. Open the PDF with Google Docs
  3. Delete the pages you don't need
  4. Download as PDF

Pros:

  • Completely free
  • Works on any device with a browser

Cons:

  • Privacy concerns (files uploaded to Google servers)
  • Formatting may change
  • Not ideal for complex PDFs

When to use: Quick deletion, non-sensitive documents.

How to Select Pages to Delete

Selection Method How to Do It
Single page Enter "5" to delete page 5 only
Multiple pages Enter "3,7,12" to delete pages 3, 7, and 12
Range Enter "5-10" to delete pages 5 through 10
Combination Enter "3,7,12-15" to delete pages 3, 7, and 12-15
All except Select "Delete all" then "Keep pages 1,2,3"
Odd/Even pages Select "Delete all even pages"

What to Consider Before Deleting

Consideration Why It Matters
Page numbers PDF page numbers may differ from printed page numbers
Bookmarks Bookmarks to deleted pages may break
Links Links to deleted pages may break
Table of contents TOC may need updating after deletion
Form fields Form fields on deleted pages are removed
